### 6.2 Label Semantics & Clinical Shift

1. **Label Family Dichotomy (`birads` vs. `biopsy`)**:
   - **`birads` Family (VinDr-Mammo, INbreast)**: Labels reflect expert consensus radiologist interpretation following the ACR BI-RADS lexicon (BI-RADS 1 = normal, 2–3 = benign, 4–5/6 = suspicious/malignant). These sites capture realistic screening and diagnostic triage distributions, including high negative/normal rates.
   - **`biopsy` Family (CMMD, CDD-CESM)**: Ground truth is anchored in histopathological biopsy confirmation. CMMD consists entirely of biopsied lesions, resulting in an enriched positive rate (~68.6% malignant).
2. **3-Class Standard Formulation**:
   - **Class 0 (Normal)**: Present in VinDr-Mammo (47.9%) and CDD-CESM (34.0%), as well as INbreast (16.3% BI-RADS 1), representing baseline uncompromised tissue.
   - **Class 1 (Benign)**: Benign cysts, fibroadenomas, and stable findings (VinDr 35.6%, CMMD 31.4%, CDD 32.9%, INbreast 59.3%).
   - **Class 2 (Suspicious / Malignant)**: Actionable malignancy requiring intervention (VinDr 16.6%, CMMD 68.6%, INbreast 24.4%, CDD 33.1%).
3. **Non-IID Heterogeneity Across Hospitals**:
   - **Scanner Differences**: Siemens Mammomat Novation / Inspiration (VinDr, INbreast), GE Senographe 2000D / DS / Essential (CMMD, CDD), Planmed Nuance, and Giotto systems.
   - **Spatial Resolution & Feature Demands**: INbreast is dominated by fine punctate microcalcifications requiring preservation of high-frequency spatial detail, whereas CMMD and CDD-CESM are predominantly mass-centric, demanding wider receptive fields and contrast discrimination.
